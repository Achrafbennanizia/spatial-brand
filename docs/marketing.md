# Dixor — marketing strategy & research brief

## Positioning
**Creative work CFOs can defend.**  
Dixor is a creative digital agency that sells *proof-led craft*: brand, product UI, and launch systems with measurable outcomes — not mood boards.

Narrow truth (sticky): *Buyers don’t need more aesthetics; they need creative they can justify.*

## Strategy: proof-led spatial funnel
Each room answers one buying stage. Scroll = progression through the funnel.

| Room | Funnel job | Buyer question | Proof type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio (hub) | Position | Why this studio? | Category + method |
| Services | Offer | What do you sell? | Deliverables tied to ROI jobs |
| Work | Evidence | Have you done this before? | Case cards with metrics |
| Clients | Trust | Will we stick / get reporting? | Retention + transparency |
| Team | Risk reduction | Who ships this? | Hiring bar + operating culture |

### Rules
1. One job per room — no mixed CTAs.
2. Prefer industry facts + Dixor operating claims; label clearly.
3. Case stories use situation → decision → result.
4. Spatial move = narrative move (camera = stage change).

## Selected research facts (integrated in product copy)
Sources used to shape claims (not invented Dixor revenue):

1. **77% of clients** stress that creative work must demonstrate ROI  
   — Design Business Council, *What Clients Think* 2024–25 (680 client interviews).

2. **75% of agency clients** run conversion-driven campaigns  
   — AgencyAnalytics, Marketing Agency Benchmarks 2024.

3. **81% of agency leaders** say strong relationships are the top retention driver; **70%** rate client reporting “extremely important”  
   — AgencyAnalytics, Benchmarks 2025.

4. Agencies typically retain clients **2–5 years**; **communication & transparency** cited as the #1 retention tactic by 46% of leaders  
   — AgencyAnalytics 2024.

5. High-performing agencies retain ~**92%** of clients annually vs ~**78%** for smaller peers  
   — Predictable Profits, Agency Growth Benchmarks 2025.

6. **Branding / content / design** demand rose **4–6 points** in recent agency industry surveys  
   — Promethean Research, Digital Agency Industry Report 2025.

7. **56%** of agency leaders say understanding client needs early is what wins pitches  
   — AgencyAnalytics 2024.

8. B2B buyers increasingly seek **evidence-backed case studies before contacting** an agency  
   — industry practice consensus (case studies as pre-sales proof).

## How facts map into the experience
- **Hub:** Lead with the ROI pressure (77%) → Dixor’s answer is proof-led rooms.
- **Services:** Align offers to conversion work (75%) + rising brand/design demand.
- **Work:** Flip boards = case proof format (situation / craft / metric).
- **Clients:** Retention & reporting stats → why partners stay.
- **Team:** Relationships + shipping cadence → who makes retention possible.

## Primary CTA ladder
1. Explore services → 2. Start a project → 3. Request case deck → 4. Apply with work  
Never ask for “contact us” without a stage-matched reason.
